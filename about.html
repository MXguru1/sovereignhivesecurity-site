About

Put AI security operations back in the hands of the operator.

Who we are

Sovereign Hive is founded by Duayne Gregg, an independent security researcher and platform architect in Queensland, Australia.

Our vision is sovereign, local-first AI security tooling that a solo researcher can run for free and an enterprise can trust air-gapped.

Values

  • The little guy stays free; the big payer pays.
  • People in distress are never monetised.
  • Defence only, with coordinated disclosure.
  • Show the work: verifiable, auditable, no invented claims.

Caitlyn’s Legacy

Sovereign Hive revenue is intended to fund Caitlyn’s Legacy, a free community mental-healthcare service.

“The tech is the vehicle. The legacy is the destination.”

Roadmap

Planned phases: a community and free release, then paid individual tiers, team tiers, and enterprise and air-gapped deployments. No dates are committed.
